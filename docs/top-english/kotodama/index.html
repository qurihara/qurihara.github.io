Kotodama

KOTODAMA: A Study of Dynamic Presentations

  1. Kazutaka Kurihara
  2. Takeo Igarashi
  3. Ken Ito


Links

movie

Most presentation systems are designed to present prepared material organized in a linear sequence of slides. While this is suitable for business communication, teaching in school requires more improvisational communication to facilitate learning. To address this issue, we have developed a dynamic presentation system that supports such improvisational communication. And we have then provided the system to teachers in local schools.

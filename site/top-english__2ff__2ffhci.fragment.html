A Page for HCI Researchers: Watching WISS and Interaction Videos at High Speed with the 2FF YouTube Viewer

This site lets you watch, at extremely high speed, all the videos of Interaction 2016, WISS 2015, Interaction 2015 and Interaction 2014 that are published on YouTube. (WISS 2014 is also published, but is excluded because it did not support the embedded YouTube player.) It has been tested with Chrome and Firefox on macOS and Windows. It works after a fashion in Safari on macOS, but not smoothly.

If you use a Chrome extension that can play at up to four times speed, this YouTube playlist lets you watch at very high speed, including WISS 2014.

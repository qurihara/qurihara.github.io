Kotodama Lecture is a tool for teaching and presenting that lets you both edit and present using only a mouse or a digital pen.

"So that even people who are not comfortable with computers, and say they find the keyboard difficult, can use it."

That is the goal of Kotodama.

Just as you write on paper with a pen. If we start from there, surely anyone can come to use a computer well.

It is currently used in lessons by teachers at public primary, junior high and high schools. (photographs)

Examples of use in Chiba Prefecture (lesson plans that move) can be seen here and here.

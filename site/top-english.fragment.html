I do research that uses information technology to surprise people. I try to keep my research open to society, so please feel free to get in touch about any of the following.

  • Invitations for talks, lectures, panels, and judging are welcome here.

    • I speak regularly at companies and universities.

  • Proposals for joint research with the Kurihara Laboratory are welcome here.

    • We have a track record, and the arrangement can take many forms.

  • Requests for interviews from television, newspapers, magazines, and web media are welcome here.

    • I do these regularly.

  • Requests for interview-based consulting are welcome here.

    • I have advised on product and service development, and have taken part in idea-generation processes over long periods.

  • Enquiries about graduate study are welcome here.

    • I accept students from outside Tsuda University into both the master's and doctoral programs. The fields I can supervise are human-computer interaction and entertainment computing. I aim to teach carefully and attentively to each student. Students I have supervised have won awards at conferences and technology contests, been selected for the IPA Exploratory Software Project and certified as Super Creators, gone on to graduate schools abroad, and been appointed as JSPS Research Fellows. A list of the laboratory students' work is available here.

SpeechJammerLite (free!)

SpeechJammerLite is a simplified version of SpeechJammer working on your PC. You can experience "artificial speech disturbance" with Delayed Auditory Feedback.


You can try with your web browser from here. 2025/10/2


All you have to do is to prepare a headset, wear it,  tune the volume up, speak something and jam yourself! You can also use "JamTimer," a presentation timer that fires speech-jamming after a specified duration.

  • Download SpeechJammerLite for Windows (DirectX is required. Install from here.)

How to use

Tips

Use another headset with a better ear-sealing performance if you can not obtain enough results.

Note that the speech inhibiting effect varies greatly with the individual and with accustomization.
